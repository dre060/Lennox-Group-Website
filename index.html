Who we are

A home for ambitious businesses.

Lennox Group brings together a family of companies under one roof. We provide the capital, leadership and shared infrastructure that let each business focus on what it does best — serving its customers exceptionally well.

We think in decades, not quarters. That means investing in people, building durable systems and making decisions that compound over time.

  • Long-term ownership with no pressure to flip
  • Shared finance, legal, talent and technology services
  • Leadership teams empowered to run their businesses
